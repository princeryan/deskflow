Detailed Description

Applies a clean, system-following theme (light/dark + accent) to the app.

Follows the desktop's colour scheme (via Qt's style hints) and accent colour (via the org.freedesktop.appearance portal), and re-applies live when either changes. The look is a spacious, rounded, "cards" style tuned for modern Ubuntu with macOS-like clarity, generated as a Qt style sheet over the Fusion style.
